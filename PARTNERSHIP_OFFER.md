# Partnership Offer: Global Content Localization

This document serves as a standard agreement template for partnering with our **TranslationTurbo** firm to localize and monetize your YouTube content in new markets.

---

## 1. The Proposition
We provide a full-cycle, studio-quality translation and dubbing service for your existing YouTube library. We manage the localized channel, handling everything from transcription to professional AI-dubbing and metadata optimization.

## 2. Revenue Share (50/50 Model)
- **Net Revenue Split:** All monthly AdSense revenue generated by the localized channel, after deducting direct processing costs (API/GPU expenses), is split **50% to the Original Creator** and **50% to the Translation Firm**.
- **Transparency:** Partners receive access to a real-time **Finance Dashboard** showing processing costs vs. gross revenue.

## 3. Copyright & Safety
- **Whitelisting:** The Original Creator agrees to whitelist the partner channel in their YouTube Content ID settings to prevent copyright strikes and enable monetization.
- **Brand Integrity:** We use advanced LLM-based content moderation to ensure all translations remain faithful to your original brand voice and platform policies.

## 4. Technical Advantages
- **Atmospheric Dubbing:** We preserve your original background music and sound effects (SFX) using advanced vocal separation.
- **Natural Sync:** No quality-degrading time-stretching. We use intelligent text-length constraints for a natural viewing experience.
- **Global Fleet:** Our distributed GPU infrastructure ensures rapid turnaround for your entire back-catalog.
