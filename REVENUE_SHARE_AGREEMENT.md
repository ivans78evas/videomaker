# Revenue Share Agreement (Template)

This Agreement is entered into between **[Translation Firm Name]** ("Firm") and **[Creator Name/Channel]** ("Partner").

## 1. Scope of Work
Firm will provide end-to-end localization services (translation, dubbing via AI voice cloning, and metadata optimization) for Partner's YouTube content.

## 2. Revenue Distribution
- **Net Profit Definition:** Gross monthly AdSense revenue from localized content minus direct processing costs (API fees, GPU compute).
- **Split:** Partner receives **50%** and Firm receives **50%** of Net Profit.
- **Reporting:** Firm will provide monthly reports from YouTube Analytics. Partner has the right to audit these figures.

## 3. Intellectual Property
- **Original Content:** Remains the exclusive property of the Partner.
- **Localized Audio:** Created by the Firm. Partner is granted a non-exclusive license to use this audio for the duration of this Agreement.
- **Voice Profile:** Cloned voice models of the Partner will be used strictly for Partner's content and deleted upon termination of this Agreement.

## 4. Technical Integration
Partner agrees to provide Firm with "Editor" or "Manager" access to the localized YouTube channels and to whitelist Firm's partner channels in Partner's Content ID settings.

## 5. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. Upon termination, Firm will cease use of Partner's voice profiles.
