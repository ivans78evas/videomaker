# Outreach Strategy: The "Zero-Risk" Global Growth Pitch

This guide outlines how to approach YouTube creators and secure localized partnership deals using the TranslationTurbo firm model.

---

## 1. The "Hook" (First Contact)
**Goal:** Offer a free "Proof of Concept" (PoC) to remove fear and show quality.

**Script Template (Telegram/Email):**
> **Subject:** Global Growth for [Channel Name] (Zero Production Cost)
>
> Hi [Creator Name],
>
> I've been following [Channel Name] and love your content on [Topic]. I'm an AI Localization Architect, and I'd like to help you reach a global audience (Spanish, German, Hindi) using YouTube’s **Multi-Language Audio (MLA)** feature.
>
> **Why work with me?**
> 1. **Voice Consistency:** We use Zero-Shot cloning, so the dubbed audio sounds exactly like you, not a robot.
> 2. **Zero Effort:** I handle the entire pipeline—translation, dubbing, and metadata optimization.
> 3. **Zero Risk:** I work on a **50/50 Revenue Share** basis. I only make money if the localized content generates revenue for you.
>
> **The Offer:**
> Let me localize one of your past videos for free. You can upload the audio track and see the engagement yourself. If you like the result, we can discuss a long-term partnership.
>
> Interested? I can send over a sample of our recent work.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

## 2. Handling Common Objections

| Objection | Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Will I get a copyright strike?"** | "No. You whitelist our channel in your Content ID, and since we use your original video with your permission, it's 100% legal." |
| **"I don't want a robotic voice."** | "We use the latest Omni-Voice technology to clone your specific tone and emotions. It sounds natural and native." |
| **"How do I know I'm getting my 50%?"** | "You have 100% visibility via YouTube Analytics. We also provide a partner dashboard showing the processing costs vs. revenue." |

---

## 3. The Onboarding Process
1. **Pilot Video:** creator sends 1 URL -> we process -> creator reviews.
2. **Partnership Agreement:** sign the 50/50 Revenue Share Agreement.
3. **Whitelisting:** Creator adds our partner channel to their whitelist.
4. **Bulk Processing:** We ingest their top 50 videos to jumpstart the localized presence.
